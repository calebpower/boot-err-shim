A font recovered from a console, not shipped with the program
=============================================================

This is real output from `boot-err-shim show-calibration --glyphs`, produced
by the containerised tier: xterm drew the PERC message in a -misc-fixed 9x15
bitmap font, TigerVNC served the pixels, and calibration worked backwards from
them to the character grid and to a bitmap for every character in the message.
Nothing here had seen that font before.

It is committed because it is the evidence for the claim the whole design
rests on. `verify: 0 px differ` says the learned glyphs are self-consistent
and can reproduce the screen they came from -- but a machine cannot tell you
they are *letters*. Look at the D, the P, the Y below and you can.

Regenerate with:  make test-e2e STAGES=calibrate
